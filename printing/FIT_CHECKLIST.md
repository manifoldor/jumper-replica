# Pilot physical fit log

Record printer/model, effective bed, nozzle, actual material, slicer/version, temperatures, layer height, support settings and date. Photograph each sample before/after support removal. Use original-coordinate components and saved transforms for comparison; record whether a measured feature is damaged by support removal.

Before changing geometry, separate global printer dimensional error from local clearance error. Record measured gap/interference and the proposed local edit in millimeters; do not apply a blanket scale correction to matched hardware. A fit sample does not validate fastener pullout, impact, fatigue or loaded walking. Foot material choice and metal connector fabrication must be resolved separately.
