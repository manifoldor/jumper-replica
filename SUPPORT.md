# Project support

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/manifoldor/jumper-replica/issues/new/choose) for geometry/reproduction problems, physical-fit measurements and focused improvement proposals. English and Chinese submissions are welcome. Include the repository commit, affected source IDs and evidence. The templates request different information for digital problems and physical measurements.

Before reporting, read [known information](docs/KNOWN_INFORMATION.md), [assembly](docs/ASSEMBLY.md) and [reproduction](docs/REPRODUCTION.md). For a code or geometry contribution, follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

This project currently provides recovered geometry and fit references. Firmware, wiring, material, tolerance and validated hardware-integration gaps are listed in the engineering status. Support does not imply a complete manufacturing kit or a response-time commitment.

Keep reports focused on public project data. Sanitize logs and photos; do not post credentials, private paths, hardware serial numbers or third-party CAD without redistribution permission.
